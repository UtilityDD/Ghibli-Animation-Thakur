## 4. CLOTHING CONSTRUCTION & LAYER RULES

1. **Inner Chest Wrap:** Unstitched white cotton cloth crossing diagonally from the left shoulder down toward the right ribs, leaving a natural triangular exposure of the right collarbone and upper chest.
2. **Outer Uttoriyo (Shawl):** Draped symmetrically over both shoulders, framing the neck with a soft vertical collar fold, hanging down over the upper arms to mid-forearm in wide, soft bell-like drapes.
3. **Dhoti (Lower Garment):**
   - **Seated (*Sukhasana*):** Covers both crossed legs generously, pooling onto the woven reed mat (*pati*) at the left and right outer knees while leaving the right bare foot and ankle exposed in front.
   - **Standing / Walking / Running:** Traditional rural Bengali child's white cotton *dhoti* draped cleanly to **knee-to-mid-calf length**, with a central vertical pleated tuck (*koncha*) that swings naturally with leg motion, and **bare feet**.

## 5. DO NOTS (IDENTITY DRIFT GUARDRAILS)

- **Never change the hair part side:** The hair part is ALWAYS on Child Thakur's **left side** (viewer's right in front view).
- **Never elongate the body:** Standing height must remain locked at **4.6 heads** (do not drift into 6-head teenager proportions or 3-head chibi proportions).
- **Never add footwear or stitched modern shirts/kurtas:** No buttons, seams, sandals, or sacred threads unless explicitly directed.
- **Never bake the background yellow halo into transparent character sprites:** The golden aura (`#FEFABD`) must be an optional background/rim layer so transparent PNG exports (`RGBA`) composite cleanly over any scene.
